RETURN TO MATRIX

F1 in Schools - Mini Formula 1 Car

Design and manufacture of a CO2-powered mini F1 car for a 20 m sprint, under strict 2024 regulations.

Project Context and Objective

This project is part of the international F1 in Schools challenge. The goal is to engineer a mini Formula 1 car propelled by an 8 g CO2 cartridge, machined from an approved F1 Model Block, and optimized to run a straight 20 m track as fast as possible while meeting the 2024 technical rules (T1 to T10).

Engineering focus: strict compliance, aerodynamic performance, rolling stability, CNC manufacturability, and professional deliverables.

01 · ENGINEERING PROCESS

An iterative design process

  1. 01 Regulations

    Identify T1–T10 constraints and required features: model block, CO₂ chamber, tether guides and wheels.

  2. 02 CAD

    Build subassemblies in Fusion 360, then refine three to four design versions.

  3. 03 CFD

    Compare flow at 50 m/s and revise wing transitions and the side skirt.

  4. 04 Prototype

    3D-print the assembly to check fit and reinforce the front axle region.

  5. 05 CAM / CNC

    Prepare roughing, ball-end finishing and the machining fixture.

  6. 06 Testing

    Compare the manufactured geometry against CAD and observe track behaviour.

Compliance checks run through every stage. CFD findings and prototype defects feed back into CAD before machining preparation.
