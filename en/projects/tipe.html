MODEL CHECK

A reference for the experiments

v2′v1=1+e1+m2m1

Ball initially at rest; external impulse neglected during contact. m₁: striker, m₂: ball, e: restitution coefficient. For m₁ ≫ m₂ and e ≈ 1, the ideal ratio approaches 2.

ENGINEERING TAKEAWAY

Comparable speed, different contact

Changing the head separated two effects: comparable launch speeds in the illustrated 12 mm / plate case, but different deformation and contact duration.

Beyond the rig: the football context

Human kicks add joint motion, timing and aiming. This biomechanical discussion, informed by LadHyX / École Polytechnique research, is separate from the pendulum measurements.
