Student Wellbeing Association Vice-President & Co-Founder · 2026

Turning a shared need into a dedicated space

There was no extracurricular initiative focused on student wellbeing on campus. Through direct conversations and surveys, our three-person founding team identified a recurring need: students wanted a quiet place to pause, decompress, or rest between classes—especially during the lunch break.

As Vice-President and Co-Founder, I helped turn that feedback into the Student Wellbeing Association and a practical outcome: a relaxation room opened at Arts et Métiers in April 2026. The project taught me that meaningful initiatives start by listening carefully, validating a need, and building the solution with others.

Team Sports Club basketball · 2017—2022

Making progress as a team

I enjoy almost every team sport and continued playing basketball recreationally after five years in a club. What matters most to me is not simply performing well individually, but helping the group improve together.

When teammates are less experienced or find it difficult to enter the game, I take time to explain, encourage, and help them build confidence. I would rather create the conditions to win together than leave someone on the margins of the team.

Long-Term Progress Strength training · Since 2022

Consistency over intensity

I train about twice a week and focus on making the practice sustainable. In sport as in other areas, I believe that doing a manageable amount consistently leads to stronger long-term progress than an intense effort that cannot be maintained.

Community Engagement

Sharing time, knowledge, and practical help

September 2022 — Present

Volunteer Tutor

I provide regular academic support to middle and high school students, primarily young people from working-class and underserved backgrounds. I help them with mathematics, physics, and engineering sciences, while also working on study methods, confidence, and learning autonomy.

For me, tutoring is not about giving an answer quickly. It is about adapting the explanation, helping the student understand the reasoning, and giving them the tools to progress independently.
